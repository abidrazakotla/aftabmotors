Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Who we are

AftabMotors makes this app for car showrooms to run their stock, sales, payments and investor deals. Anyone can make an account and create a showroom; its owner invites the staff, and approves each one with a role.

Each showroom decides what it records about its own business and the people it deals with, and is responsible for having the right to record it. We keep it for them and use it only to run the app.

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: brothershubpk@gmail.com.

What the app keeps

Your account: your email, your name, your role in each showroom, and your sign-in. Passwords are kept only in scrambled form by the sign-in service. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your email (or a private relay address) and, the first time, your name.

When you ask to join a showroom, its owner sees your name and email to decide.

The showroom's records, as its staff enter them: cars (make, model, registration, chassis and engine numbers, prices), the people the showroom deals with (names, phone numbers and city), payments, costs, sales, investor agreements and settlements, and photos of cars, receipts and papers.

An audit trail: which account recorded what, and when.

The app doesn't collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or usage or crash analytics. It shows no ads and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

How it's used

Only to run the app: to show the showroom's records to its staff, according to each person's role; to check every entry against the showroom's money rules before it's saved; and to keep the audit trail.

Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with data brokers.

Where it's kept

On the showroom's server, run for us by Supabase on servers in Mumbai, India. Everything travels encrypted. Photos and papers are in private storage that only the showroom's staff can open.

Your iPhone or Mac keeps a copy of the records it has been shown, so they open without a connection. The copy is in the app's own storage, and is removed when your account is deleted.

Who can see it

The showroom's own staff, by role: investor capital and profit only to the owner, managers and accountants, and the audit trail only to owners.

Supabase (the server) and Apple (Sign in with Apple) handle data only to provide their service to us.

Records leave the showroom only when its staff share a statement or report themselves, or when the law requires it.

How long it's kept

The showroom's records are kept for as long as the showroom uses the app: they're its business records. Its book is never edited; a correction is added as a new entry that reverses the old one.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings › Delete Account. If you're not staff in any showroom, Delete Account is also on the screen you see after signing in.

Deleted at once: your sign-in, your name and email from the showroom's staff, any requests to join you made, and everything the app keeps on your iPhone or Mac.

If you're a showroom's only owner, that showroom is deleted too, with every record, photo and paper in it. The app shows this and asks you to type the showroom's name first.

An owner can also delete a showroom and keep their account: Settings › Delete This Showroom. Every record, photo and paper in it is deleted, for all its staff.

Kept: in a showroom that carries on, the records you made and its audit trail stay, as the showroom's business records.

People in a showroom's records

Customers, sellers and investors recorded by a showroom can ask that showroom, or us at brothershubpk@gmail.com, to see, correct or delete their details. Some records must be kept by the showroom as business and tax records.

Children

The app is for showroom staff, not for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is shown here with its date.

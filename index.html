Getting started

Make an account. Use an email and password or Sign in with Apple, then choose Create a Showroom. You become its owner.

Add your team. In the menu, open Staff & Access, make an invite code for a role and share it. Your staff enter it under Join a Showroom, and you approve each one.

Common questions

I forgot my password. If you made your account with Sign in with Apple, use it again: it needs no password. Otherwise email us from your sign-in address and we will help you back in.

I recorded something wrong. Entries are never edited. Open the entry and reverse it, then record it again correctly. Both stay in the audit trail.

Someone left the showroom. The owner can switch off their access under Staff & Access. The records they made stay.

Does it work without a connection? The app keeps a copy of your records on the device, so you can read them offline.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Menu, Settings, Delete Account. It is deleted at once. If you are a showroom's only owner, that showroom and its records are deleted with it, after you type its name. See the privacy policy for what is kept.
